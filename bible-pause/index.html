Bible Pause

Pray first. Scroll second. Bible Pause puts a short prayer and a Bible verse between you and the apps that distract you, so reaching for your phone becomes a moment with God.

A pause before you scroll

Choose the apps that pull you in. When you open one, Bible Pause asks you to check in and pray before you continue.

Prayer and Scripture, daily

A new Bible verse each day, a prayer for how you feel, and a streak and history that show the habit growing.

Private by design

No account and no ads. Your prayers and history are stored on your iPhone. Personalised AI prayers are optional and off until you allow them.
