This policy explains what data the Bible Pause app for iPhone uses, where it is stored and who it is shared with. Bible Pause is published by FOURPLEX SIA, Latvia ("we", "us"; see Contact). For the data that reaches us or the services we use, we are the controller under the GDPR and UK GDPR.

Summary

  • No account. You never give us an email or password. The first name you enter stays on your iPhone.
  • Your prayers and history are stored on your iPhone. Your answers, check-ins, prayers, verses and streak are kept on your phone.
  • AI prayers are optional. Only if you allow it, some of your answers are sent to our server and to Google Gemini to write a prayer. Your name is never sent. You can turn this off at any time.
  • We never learn which apps you block. Apple's Screen Time keeps your app selection on your phone.
  • No ads, no tracking, no data sales. We do not use the advertising identifier, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

1. What stays on your phone

This data is stored on your iPhone and is not sent to us. The only exception is the optional feature described in section 3.

DataWhat it is
ProfileThe answers you give when you set up the app: first name, age range, gender, faith tradition, goals, struggles, spiritual strengths, how often you pray and how you describe your relationship with God. Stored in the iPhone Keychain.
Check-insHow you say you feel and how close you feel to God each time you pause.
Prayers and versesThe prayers you have prayed, the verses you have seen, and when.
Streak and statisticsThe days you prayed and the totals shown in Your Journey and in the home screen widgets.
Blocking settingsYour selection of apps to pause, your unlock time and whether blocking is on. See section 2.

Some of these answers describe your religious beliefs. We treat them as sensitive: they stay on your phone unless you allow personalised AI prayers.

Notifications are optional. They are local notifications created on your phone, for example to tell you that your apps are paused or that a free trial is ending. We do not send push notifications from a server.

Photos. If you save an image of your journey, the app asks for permission to add it to your photo library. It cannot read your photos.

2. Screen Time and app blocking

Bible Pause uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) to pause the apps you choose. You pick them in a screen provided by Apple. Apple gives the app private tokens instead of app names, so we cannot see which apps you chose, and neither the selection nor your usage of other apps ever leaves your phone. We do not read your Screen Time usage data.

Screen Time access is optional. You can remove it at any time in iPhone Settings › Screen Time, and the prayer and verse features keep working.

3. Personalised AI prayers

Bible Pause can write a prayer for the way you feel today. This feature is off until you allow it. The app asks you before the first personalised prayer, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Personalised AI prayers.

If you allow it, each time a new prayer is written the app sends:

  • your check-in: how you feel and how close you feel to God;
  • your faith tradition;
  • the struggles and goals you selected.

This goes to our server, hosted on Amazon Web Services in the USA, which passes it to Google's Gemini API to generate the prayer text. Your name, your app ID and your blocked apps are never sent. As with any internet request, your IP address is visible to our server.

Our server does not store your answers or the prayer. The prayer is saved only on your phone. Google processes the request under its Gemini API terms.

If you do not allow it, or turn it off, nothing is sent and you pray a classic prayer stored in the app.

Bible Pause contains no third-party analytics or advertising software other than the services listed above.

We may disclose data when the law requires it, for example because of a court order, or to protect our rights or someone's safety. If Bible Pause is sold or merged, this policy continues to apply to your data.

5. Why we use your data (legal basis)

  • Personalised AI prayers: your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings. Because these answers can reveal religious beliefs, we rely on explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a) GDPR.
  • Subscriptions and paywalls: performance of our contract with you, and our legitimate interest in understanding how our subscription offers perform.
  • Accounting records: legal obligation.

We only use data for the purposes in this policy. If we ever want to use it for something new, we will ask you first.

6. Tracking, advertising and sales

Bible Pause shows no ads. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and do not combine your data with data from other companies.

We do not sell or "share" personal data, as those terms are used in US state privacy laws, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

8. Your choices and rights

Your choices

  • AI prayers: turn them on or off in Settings › Personalised AI prayers. Turning them off stops any further sending; a request already made cannot be recalled.
  • Screen Time and notifications: change them in iPhone Settings.
  • Delete data on your phone: use Settings › Delete account. This erases your profile, prayers, check-ins, history and app selection. Deleting the app alone removes your prayers and history, but iOS may keep your profile in the Keychain, so use Delete account first.
  • Delete data our providers hold: email us. Because there are no accounts, subscription records are linked only to a random app ID.
  • Subscription: deleting your data or the app does not cancel a subscription. Cancel in iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, ask us to restrict processing, object to processing, withdraw consent and ask for a copy in a portable format.

If you live in a US state with a privacy law (such as California), you have similar rights to know, delete and correct, and to opt out of sales or sharing, which we do not do. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

Email contact@fourplex.org. We reply within one month. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one in your country.

9. Transfers outside the EU

Superwall, Amazon Web Services and Google are US companies. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (with the UK addendum for UK users).

10. Security

Your profile is stored in the iPhone Keychain, and your prayers and history in the app's private storage on your phone. All network traffic uses HTTPS. Our providers process data under contracts that require them to protect it. If a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Children

Bible Pause is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age for consenting to data processing in your country, if higher). We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has used the app, email us and we will delete any data we hold.

12. Changes

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top. We will tell you in the app before important changes take effect, such as a new service that receives your data.
