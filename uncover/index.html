An app for iPhone by FOURPLEX

Uncover

Your city is hidden. Go uncover it. Uncover covers the map in fog and clears it wherever you go, one street at a time, and shows how much of your neighbourhood and city you've explored.

Street by street

Walk, run, cycle or drive, and every street you pass lights up. Watch your city's percentage climb.

Somewhere new, every day

Daily discoveries, side quests into the fog and a streak for new ground make exploring a habit.

Private by design

No account. Your routes are stored encrypted on your iPhone and only leave it when you choose.
