This policy explains what data the Uncover app for iPhone uses, where it is stored and who it is shared with. Uncover is published by FOURPLEX SIA, Latvia ("we", "us"; see Contact). For the data that reaches us or the services we use, we are the controller under the GDPR and UK GDPR.

Summary

  • No account. You never give us your name, email or password.
  • Your map stays on your iPhone. Routes, coverage, places and history are kept in an encrypted database on your phone. We cannot see it.
  • Your routes are never sent to us or anyone else. Only what a feature needs leaves your phone, such as a search term or the map area on screen.
  • No ads, no tracking, no data sales. We do not use the advertising identifier, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.
  • Analytics are optional. You can turn them off in Settings › Privacy.

1. What stays on your phone

This data is stored only in an encrypted database on your iPhone. We cannot access it.

DataWhat it is
LocationWith your permission, your precise location while you explore. If you turn on Auto-reveal, also in the background, using low-power location that pauses when you're still.
Explorations and routesRoutes, distances, times, transport mode and the parts of the map you have uncovered.
Places and areasYour home city, tracked cities, neighbourhoods and custom areas, saved spots and notes, saved routes, places visited.
PhotosWith your permission, the location and date of photos you choose, used to add them to your map. Photos are never uploaded or copied.
Apple HealthWith your permission, the routes, dates and type of the walking, running and cycling workouts you select. See section 2.
Imported filesGPX files and Google Timeline exports you choose to import. They are processed on your phone.
SettingsYour preferences, onboarding answers, home privacy zone and offline map areas.

Every permission is optional. If you say no, the rest of the app keeps working. You can change permissions at any time in iPhone Settings › Uncover.

Turn-by-turn voice prompts are spoken by your iPhone and are not sent anywhere. Reminders are local notifications created on your phone; we do not send push notifications.

2. Apple Health data

Uncover only reads the routes of the workouts you select, to draw them on your map. It never writes to Apple Health.

Health data:

  • stays in the encrypted database on your phone;
  • is never sent to us, our analytics or our subscription provider;
  • is never used for advertising, marketing or analytics, or by any AI service;
  • is never sold or shared with anyone.

3. Backups and exports

If you turn on iCloud Backup, an encrypted copy of your database is saved in your own iCloud Drive, protected by a password you choose. iCloud backups never include data imported from Apple Health; after restoring, you can import those workouts again. You can also export a backup file, GPX files or share images yourself. We cannot read your backups. Without the password, a backup cannot be restored. You can delete iCloud backups in iPhone Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Share images skip your home privacy zone and hidden areas and contain no location data. What you share, and with whom, is up to you.

4. What leaves your phone

Some features need to contact other services. Each one receives only what it needs.

ServiceWhyWhat it receives
Uncover map server (run by us on Cloudflare) Map themes, offline maps and place search Map tiles for the area on screen or the areas you download. For search: what you type, or your location rounded to about 100 m (for example, for the "You're in" suggestion), and your language. Also your IP address and a random ID that changes every time you open the app, used only to prevent abuse.
LocationIQ (our place-search provider) Place search and city boundaries Search terms and rounded locations passed on by our server. It never receives your IP address or device details.
Apple Classic maps, search, directions, payments, iCloud The map area you view, what you search for, route start and end points, purchases and backups. If "Name places I've been" is on: approximate centres of areas you visited, to name them. Apple processes this under its own privacy policy.
Superwall (USA) Subscriptions, trials and paywalls The random app ID, device model, iOS version, language, IP address, subscription status, the name of your home city and rounded progress numbers (for example, % of your city uncovered, number of cities visited).
Amplitude (USA) Usage analytics The random app ID, device model, iOS version, app version and usage events in broad ranges (for example, "exploration finished, 3–5 km"). Your IP address is not stored.

Our map server does not store your searches or locations in its logs. To keep the app fast, search results are cached for up to 30 days, keyed only by the search term or rounded location, with no link to you or your device.

We never send your routes, exact coordinates or place notes to analytics or to Superwall. We do not send any of your data to AI services.

We may disclose data when the law requires it, for example because of a court order, or to protect our rights or someone's safety. If Uncover is sold or merged, this policy continues to apply to your data.

5. Why we use your data (legal basis)

  • To provide the app (maps, search, subscriptions, abuse prevention): performance of our contract with you, and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.
  • Accounting records: legal obligation.
  • Usage analytics: our legitimate interest in understanding how the app is used and improving it. You can object at any time by turning off analytics (section 8).

We only use data for the purposes in this policy. If we ever want to use it for something new, we will ask you first.

6. Tracking, advertising and sales

Uncover shows no ads. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and do not combine your data with data from other companies.

We do not sell or "share" personal data, as those terms are used in US state privacy laws, and we do not use it for targeted advertising. We do not collect precise location or health data on our servers.

8. Your choices and rights

Your choices

  • Analytics: turn off Settings › Privacy › Share usage analytics. Nothing more is sent after that.
  • Location, Photos, Health, notifications: change them in iPhone Settings › Uncover. Turning off Always location stops Auto-reveal; explorations still work while the app is open.
  • Delete data on your phone: delete single explorations, places or imports, or erase everything, in Settings › Your data. Deleting the app also deletes the database.
  • Delete data we or our providers hold: email us with the app ID shown in Settings › Privacy. Because there are no accounts, this ID is the only way we can find your data.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, ask us to restrict processing, object to processing and ask for a copy in a portable format. You can export your map and history yourself in Settings › Your data.

If you live in a US state with a privacy law (such as California), you have similar rights to know, delete and correct, and to opt out of sales or sharing, which we do not do. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

Email contact@fourplex.org. We reply within one month. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one in your country.

9. Transfers outside the EU

Superwall, Amplitude and LocationIQ are US companies, and Cloudflare serves the app from data centres worldwide. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (with the UK addendum for UK users).

10. Security

Data on your phone is encrypted with a key stored in the iPhone Keychain. Backups are encrypted with your password. All network traffic uses HTTPS. Only our team can access analytics and subscription data, and our providers process it under contracts that require them to protect it. If a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Children

Uncover is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age for consenting to data processing in your country, if higher). We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has used the app, email us and we will delete their data.

12. Changes

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top. We will tell you in the app before important changes take effect, such as a new service that receives your data.
