Location and the fog

The fog isn't clearing when I explore

Check that Uncover can use your location: iPhone Settings › Uncover › Location. Choose While Using the App or Always, and turn on Precise Location. With approximate location, Uncover can't tell which streets you walked. Then start an exploration from the map.

Will it keep recording when my screen is locked?

Yes, if Record when locked is on in Settings › Exploring. iOS will ask you to allow location Always for this.

What is Auto-reveal, and does it drain my battery?

Auto-reveal is an Uncover Pro feature that clears the fog wherever you go, without pressing Start. It uses low-power location that pauses while you're still, so the battery impact is small. You can turn it off any time in Settings.

My distance or city percentage looks wrong

GPS can be inaccurate near tall buildings, in tunnels or with a weak signal, so some streets may be missed or drawn slightly off. City and neighbourhood boundaries come from OpenStreetMap and may differ from official ones. If your map looks out of sync, go to Settings › Privacy & data › Your data › Rebuild map. It redraws the fog from your history and deletes nothing.

Can I fix the map by hand?

Yes. The map editor lets you reveal or erase areas and hide parts you don't want on share posters. Your recorded explorations are not changed.

Uncover Pro

What does Uncover Pro include?

Every map theme, offline maps, Auto-reveal, importing your past, unlimited places, side quests, extra streak freezes and posters without the watermark. Plans and prices are shown in the app before you buy.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Uncover and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

How does the free trial work?

If your plan includes a free trial, you won't be charged until it ends. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends if you don't want to continue.

I paid but Pro isn't unlocked

Go to Settings › Help › Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe. If it still doesn't work, email us.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so only Apple can issue refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Backups and new iPhones

Is my map backed up?

Uncover has no account, and your map is stored only on your iPhone. To keep it safe, turn on iCloud Backup in Settings › Privacy & data, or save a backup file in Settings › Privacy & data › Your data › Export backup. Both are protected by a password you choose.

How do I move my map to a new iPhone?

Install Uncover on the new iPhone and restore your iCloud backup, or use Your data › Restore from file with a backup file. Enter the password you chose when you made the backup. iCloud backups don't include data imported from Apple Health, so import those workouts again after restoring.

I forgot my backup password

We can't help with this, sorry. Backups are encrypted with your password, and we never see it. Without it, a backup can't be opened.

Importing your past

What can I import?

The places in your photos, walking, running and cycling workouts from Apple Health, GPX files from other apps, and your Google Timeline history. Everything is processed on your iPhone, and you see a preview before anything changes.

How do I get my Google Timeline file?

Open Google Maps on the phone that has your Timeline.

  • iPhone: profile › Settings › Personal content › Export Timeline data.
  • Android: Settings › Location › Location services › Timeline › Export.

Save the file, then choose it in Uncover's import screen.

Some workouts or photos don't appear

Uncover can only import workouts that have a recorded route, and photos that have a location. Also check that Uncover has access: iPhone Settings › Uncover › Photos, and iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Uncover. Importing the same item twice is skipped automatically.

Privacy and your data

Can you see where I've been?

No. Your routes and map are stored encrypted on your iPhone, and there's no account. Read the full Privacy Policy.

How do I keep my home private?

Use Settings › Privacy & data › Hide my home. Share posters then hide the area around your home.

How do I delete my data?

In Settings › Privacy & data › Your data you can delete single explorations and imports, delete all location data, or erase everything. Deleting the app also deletes its data from your iPhone. To delete iCloud backups, use the option in the app or iPhone Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

How do I turn off analytics?

Turn off Settings › Privacy & data › Share usage analytics. Analytics never include your location or places.
